## Entry Points for Contributors

**Start anywhere. Fork freely. No permission needed.**

### Story Entry Points

- Write a scene where a character discovers a map that erases a familiar landmark, replacing it with a personal memory.
- Create a dialogue between a ghost of a colonial official and a local child, where they argue over the 'true' name of a hill.
- Describe a ritual where the people of a town systematically 'un-name' a colonial building, giving each piece of it back to the elements.
- Write a letter from Elias's mother to a friend in Paris, describing the 'glorious forgetting' of the English garden, 20 years after her arrival.

### Technical Entry Points

- Build an interactive map where users can 'draw' new borders over a satellite image, with each stroke generating a micro-story of displacement or belonging.
- Create a text generator that blends colonial administrative language with surreal, organic imagery (e.g., 'The minutes of the meeting tasted of cloves and decay.').
